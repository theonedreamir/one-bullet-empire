# ONE BULLET EMPIRE

A monochrome one-projectile arcade survival game. Just for fun. Made.

Your only shot never despawns: it ricochets through enemy waves, accelerates on impact, builds a score chain, and can end your own run.

## Play

- Drag anywhere or use `A` / `D` and arrow keys to move.
- On touch screens, use the left/right pulse controls or drag.
- Keep the projectile inside the arena and away from your ship.
- Angle ricochets through enemies. Perfect-angle hits earn a larger chain increase.
- Survive escalating arenas and beat the best score stored on your device.

## Stack

TypeScript, React, HTML5 Canvas, CSS and the Web Audio API. No backend, AI, tracking, ads, lootboxes or third-party game assets.

## Design

Strict black, white and grayscale. The art direction combines 1990s vector arcade displays with editorial typography, subtle scanlines, phosphor-style afterimages, geometric silhouettes and sharp impact flashes.

## Run locally

The gameplay is self-contained in `src/App.tsx` and `src/style.css`. The entry router in `src/main.tsx` uses a platform-specific import; swap in your own bundler entry to run it anywhere.
